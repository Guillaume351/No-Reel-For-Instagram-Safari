## Bundle — English

Name: Social Media : Dopamine Detox

Description:
Keep the useful parts of your social feeds with three Safari extensions, in one purchase.

• No Shorts: hide YouTube Shorts and choose whether to hide the home feed, recommendations and comments.
• No Reel For Instagram: hide selected Reels surfaces, Stories, discovery shortcuts and suggested accounts on Instagram Web.
• No Algorithm For X: keep the Following feed and hide selected distractions on X Web.

These extensions work on the supported websites in Safari. They do not change the official YouTube, Instagram or X apps. Enable each extension and allow access to its website after installation. Device compatibility varies by included app; check each listing before purchasing.

One purchase. No subscription. BrowseKind Plus is a separate product and is not included. See the App Store for your price and any available bundle completion price.
