# No Reel For Instagram Privacy Statement

_Last updated: 2026-07-26_

## Overview

No Reel For Instagram is a Safari extension for instagram.com. The app does not require a separate app account and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- No Instagram browsing history, login credentials, social graph, or page content
- No advertising identifier or cross-app tracking data
- No usage analytics or third-party crash reporting

The extension does not send Instagram page content or extension activity to a developer-operated API.

## Extension Settings

Your filter choices are stored through Safari’s WebExtension storage APIs. The extension prefers synchronized browser storage when Safari makes it available and falls back to local browser storage. The host apps do not read or manage these settings, and this app does not directly access iCloud Key-Value Storage or an app-group container.

You can change or reset the filters from the extension popup in Safari. Removing the extension removes access to those preferences according to Safari’s storage behavior.

## Safari Extension Behavior

The extension reads and modifies the visible structure of instagram.com in the current browser tab so it can hide the surfaces you selected. It does not intercept, log, or forward Instagram network traffic. Its filtering code runs locally inside Safari’s extension sandbox.

## Host Apps and Installation Tutorial

The iOS and macOS host apps provide activation instructions. The iOS onboarding can load an optional installation video from `nextcloud.d1.guillaumeclaverie.com`. Loading or opening that video makes a standard web request to that server, which can include technical connection information such as an IP address and user agent. The video is not used for analytics or advertising.

## Third-Party Services

Apple processes App Store purchases and distribution under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. Instagram processes your use of instagram.com independently. No Reel For Instagram does not receive your Instagram account data from either service.

## Security

- Uses Apple’s app and Safari extension sandboxes
- Requests website access only for `instagram.com` and `m.instagram.com`
- Stores filter settings using Safari WebExtension storage

## Your Choices

You can disable the extension, revoke its website access, change individual filters, or uninstall it at any time from Safari or Apple system settings.
